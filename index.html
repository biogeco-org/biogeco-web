01 / ABOUT THE NETWORK

Connecting knowledge
across borders.

BioGeCo is a network for scientific and innovation-focused cooperation between Colombia and Germany. It brings researchers together across disciplines and encourages collaborative initiatives that support sustainable development.

The network welcomes exchange between scientific communities and helps make research relevant beyond academia.

05 / OUR STORY

How the network
has evolved.

Key milestones presented on the official BioGeCo website.

  1. Mar 2019

    Colombia Day at BMBF

    An early milestone linking Colombian and German scientific communities.

  2. Jul 2019

    Summer school workshop preparation

    Preparatory work for a graduate programme summer school in Cali, Colombia.

  3. Nov 2019

    Bioeconomy workshop in Cali

    A workshop exploring new value chains between Germany and Colombia in the context of the UN Sustainable Development Goals.

  4. Dec 2021

    “Vision BioGeCo”

    A virtual workshop supported by Minciencias Colombia.

  5. Nov 2022

    Outreach and collaboration

    Collaborative efforts began with the internationalisation team at Minciencias.

  6. May 2023

    Recognition of young scientists abroad

    A milestone involving the Colombian scientific diaspora in Germany and the Colombian Ambassador in Bonn.

  7. Dec 2023

    First stakeholder workshop

    BioGeCo held a stakeholder workshop hosted by the Colombian Embassy in Berlin.
